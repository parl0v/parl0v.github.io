LudusCtrl

Use a Bluetooth gamepad on a PC that has no Bluetooth radio. Your phone does the pairing and forwards input over your own Wi-Fi to a small receiver on Windows. No video streaming, nothing leaves your network.

Get it

Download for Windows Android app on Google Play

Version 1.0 · 15.3 MB · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installer sets up the ViGEmBus controller driver for you if it is not already present. SHA-256: ca9588e951590035fa829ca0c9dfa4454a19e64e560d0051ab74f880861e42f3

How it works

  • Pair your gamepad to your phone over Bluetooth as usual.
  • Install the receiver on your PC and leave it running.
  • Open the app, pick your PC, and start the link. The pad shows up in Windows as a standard controller.
