Privacy Policy — LudusCtrl

Last updated: 1 September 2026

LudusCtrl does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

LudusCtrl is developed by Josip Parlov. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. It is short because the app collects nothing.

What the app does

LudusCtrl forwards input from a gamepad paired to your device over Bluetooth to a computer on your own local network. To do this it:

  • Reads button and stick input from a gamepad you have paired, using Android's standard input APIs.
  • Sends that input as UDP packets to an IP address on your local network, either discovered automatically or typed in by you.
  • Listens for a discovery broadcast from the receiver program running on your computer.

All of this traffic stays on your local network. Nothing is sent to the developer, and nothing is sent to any internet server.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the last IP address and port you used, and whether you enabled dim-screen mode, so you do not have to set them again. This is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
INTERNET Opens a UDP socket to send input to your PC on the local network. Despite the name, Android requires this for any network socket, including local-only ones.
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE Reads the local network address so the app can find the receiver.
CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE Holds a multicast lock so discovery broadcasts are not dropped while the Wi-Fi radio is in power-save mode.

The app requests no location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries.
